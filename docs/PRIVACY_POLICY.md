# SpatialTouch Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

SpatialTouch ("the app") lets you control your Android phone with hand gestures
in front of the front camera. This policy explains what the app accesses and why.

## The short version

SpatialTouch collects **no personal data**. Nothing you do in the app leaves your
phone. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

## Camera

When gesture control is on, the app reads frames from the front camera and runs
Google MediaPipe hand tracking **on the device**. Frames are processed in memory
and discarded immediately. They are never saved to storage and never sent over
the network. A notification is always shown while the camera service is running.

If you record a custom gesture, the app stores the positions of 21 points on your
hand (numbers only, not an image) on your phone so it can recognise that pose.

## Accessibility Service

The app's Accessibility Service performs taps, swipes, scrolls and system actions
(Back, Home, Recents, Screenshot) when you make a gesture you've assigned to them.
It does not read, collect or transmit the content of your screen or what you type.

## Usage access

With your permission, the app checks which app is currently open so per-app
gesture profiles can apply. Only the current app's package name is read, it is
used immediately on the device, and it is not stored or shared.

## Other permissions

- **Display over other apps** (optional): shows the floating status indicator.
- **Notifications**: the service status notification and a reminder to resume
  after your phone restarts.
- **Vibrate**: haptic feedback when a gesture is recognised.

## Data stored on your phone

Your settings, gesture-to-action assignments, custom gesture landmarks and
gesture counts are stored locally. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data
deletes all of it.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect any data from anyone.
